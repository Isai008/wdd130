Paraguay

Paraguay is a landlocked country in the heart of South America, bordered by Argentina, Brazil, and Bolivia. Its capital city is Asunción. Paraguay is known for its rich cultural heritage, friendly people, and beautiful natural landscapes, including rivers, forests, and waterfalls. Its two official languages are Spanish and Guaraní , reflecting its strong Indigenous roots.
